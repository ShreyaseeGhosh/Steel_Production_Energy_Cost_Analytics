## Dashboard Analysis

### Production Analysis
In this project, I've performed production analysis to analyze production trends, target achievement, plant performance, furnace performance, and 
shift-wise production.

### Energy Analysis
Performed total energy consumption, energy cost, energy consumption per ton, and furnace-level energy efficiency analysis

### Cost Analysis
Performed production cost, cost per ton, raw material cost, labor cost, maintenance cost, and energy cost analysis.

### Cost Analysis
Analyzed production cost, cost per ton, raw material cost, labor cost, maintenance cost, and energy cost.

## To do so I used the following Power BI Features:
- Power Query ETL
- Data Modeling
- DAX Measures
- KPI Cards
- Interactive Slicers
- Drill-through
- Tooltips
- Conditional Formatting
- Time-based Analysis

### Outcome
The dashboard provides an integrated view of steel production, energy efficiency, cost, quality, and downtime, enabling users to identify inefficient
production areas and support data-driven operational decisions.
